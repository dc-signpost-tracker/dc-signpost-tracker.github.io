Where power, water and land redirect the data centre build-out

The outlook for physical constraint severity in select regions till 2030 and beyond, tested against 38 signposts

How to read this

Ratings

RatingMeaning
SevereLikely to delay or re-route deployment at scale
ModerateAdds cost or time, but manageable with mitigation
MinorNot a differentiating constraint

Up to 2030, ratings are capped by what is built, under construction or firmly planned. Beyond 2030, they depend on whether mitigation is delivered.

Signals

SignalMeaning
Constraint tighteningA development past the signpost's trigger that makes the constraint harder
Constraint easingA development past the trigger that makes the constraint easier
No signal yetNothing has crossed the trigger since the ratings were set on 12 Nov 2026

A call is marked Rating under review as soon as one signal runs against it: an easing signal on a Persisting or Intensifying call, or a tightening signal on an Easing call. On a call holding at Moderate, a signal in either direction counts. Ratings change only after a manual review.
