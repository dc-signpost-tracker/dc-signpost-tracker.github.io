The outlook for physical constraint severity in select regions till 2030 and beyond, tested against 24 signposts

How to read this

Ratings

RatingMeaning
SevereLikely to delay or re-route deployment at scale
ModerateAdds cost or time, but manageable with mitigation
MinorNot a differentiating constraint

Up to 2030, ratings are capped by what is built, under construction or firmly planned. Beyond 2030, they depend on whether mitigation is delivered.

Signpost status

Call typeSupports the call whenChallenges the call when
EasingThe easing is confirmedThe rating risks reverting to Severe
PersistingThe constraint holdsThere is a case for lowering severity
IntensifyingThe intensification is confirmedThe intensification is contained

A call is marked Rating under review as soon as one of its signposts challenges it. Ratings change only after a manual review.

How the data refreshes

  • Time series (5): refreshed from published data on a schedule.
  • Events (19): logged when a decision, ruling or filing lands.
  • Every verified reading links to its primary source and retrieval date. Readings without a link are marked as not yet verified.
  • New readings publish automatically. A change of status waits for a manual review.
  • Paid sources are listed but switched off in this version.

Open data issues

  • Press-only readings: some readings rest on press reports and carry a note saying so. They are replaced as primary documents are checked.
  • Texas "approved to energise": shown as capacity approved plus capacity already energised. This reading of the figures is provisional.
  • History charts for the time series are being built as readings are verified.
